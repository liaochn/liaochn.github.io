## About me

I am a graduate student studying social sciences and I love to learn new technologies. I think social science and AI technology will collide in the future. I am now focusing on the field of Computational Social Science (CSS).
Although I used to be exposed to computers and programming when I was an undergraduate, more often than not, I focused more on business than technology when I was working on startup projects with my classmates. My startup partner entered the Internet business early, and I found the gap between me and him getting wider and wider. Due to the great popularity of ChatGPT and DeepSeek and the fact that I've always had the idea of building my own website, I spent two days building this website with these two AI partners.

I also thought about using a website builder such as jekyll, although they can publish it in ten minutes with a few modifications following the documentation guidelines. But I realized that none of these templates were the way I wanted them to look, and modifying their documentation created one new problem after another. In fact, I don't think I could use a lot of the complexity either.Eventually, after deleting my project library several times, I decided to write my website from scratch, code by code. 

I used the dumbest way possible to build my academic and blogging site, using just html, css, js, and markdown, and utilizing html to manage my numerous md files. It does seem so minimalistic, but the two-day process has made me incredibly happy.
